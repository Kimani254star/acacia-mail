About Acacia Mail

A mailbox that keeps the work beside the mail.

Acacia Mail is a full webmail workspace for your company. Your inbox sits next to the things you reach for while you read: a calendar, your contacts, your tasks and your notes.

Everything you need to keep a busy inbox under control is built in: folders and labels, bulk actions you can undo, and search that understands terms like from:, has:attachment and is:unread.

Acacia Mail is a companion app for Acacia Books and is included free with every Acacia Books plan.

What you get

Everything a company mailbox needs

Six things that make a busy inbox easier to run.

Folders, labels & bulk actions

Sort mail into folders, tag it with labels, and act on many messages at once: mark read, star, archive, mark as spam, delete or move. Every bulk action can be undone.

Advanced search

Combine terms such as from:, to:, subject:, has:attachment, is:unread, is:starred, before:, after: and label: to find a message quickly. Press / to jump straight to the search box.

Compose & schedule send

Write with a rich-text editor, keep drafts as you go, and schedule a message to send later. Press c anywhere to start a new message.

Calendar

A calendar sits in the side panel next to your inbox, so dates are in view while you read and reply.

Contacts, tasks & notes

Keep your contacts, a task list and quick notes in the same workspace as your mail, so follow-ups don't get lost between apps.

Light and dark modes

Switch between a light and a dark theme with one click, whichever is easier on your eyes.

Good questions

Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers before you sign up.

What does Acacia Mail cost?

It's included free with every Acacia Books plan.

How do I create an account?

Choose Create mailbox and enter your company name, your name, your email address and a password of at least 6 characters. To join an existing company, enter its company code as well.

How does search work?

Type plain words, or combine terms like from:, to:, subject:, has:attachment, is:unread, is:starred, before:, after: and label:. For example, from:john has:attachment is:unread.

Are there keyboard shortcuts?

Yes. Press / to jump to search and c to compose a new message. Escape closes the composer.

Can I schedule a message to send later?

Yes. The composer supports drafts and schedule send.
